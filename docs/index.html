Tips

  • Keep your hand inside the white box on the left of the picture.
  • Hold a letter steady for about half a second.
  • For J and Z, draw the letter in the air inside the box, taking about one second.
  • Lower or remove your hand between repeated letters (for example L-L).
  • Use a plain background and good light.
